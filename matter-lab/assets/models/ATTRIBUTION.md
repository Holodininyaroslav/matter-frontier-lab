# Visual-asset attribution

## Black-hole accretion disk

`nasa-svs-black-hole-accretion-disk.gif` is the local copy of **NASA Scientific
Visualization Studio ID 13326**, *Black Hole Accretion Disk Visualization*.

- Visualizer: **Jeremy Schnittman** (NASA/GSFC)
- Credit: **NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center / Jeremy Schnittman**
- Source: <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13326/>

The asset is shown as NASA's scientific visualization of a thin accretion disk
around a spherical, non-rotating black hole. It must not be represented as this
project's own ray-tracing or numerical-relativity simulation.
