# CompOSE DS(CMF)-2

- Source: https://compose.obspm.fr/eos/181
- Download: https://compose.obspm.fr/download/1D/NS/RMF/CMF2/no_crust/eos.zip
- Retrieved: 2026-09-02
- SHA-256 (`eos.zip`): `197BA8EB630D93EFE7D474DEDF2AE449AD0F3ABCE43B3763507313303AE4B0E5`
- Grid: cold beta-equilibrated matter, T = 0 MeV, n_b = 0.03–3.03 fm^-3, 301 points

The data are the CompOSE distribution of the DS(CMF)-2 chiral mean-field
equation of state. Cite the CompOSE database and the model publications listed
in the archive's `README` and data sheet when using numerical results.

The application reads the original `eos.nb` and `eos.thermo` files without
altering their values. Derived pressure and energy density follow the CompOSE
definitions `P = n_b Q1` and `epsilon = n_b m_n (Q7 + 1)`.
